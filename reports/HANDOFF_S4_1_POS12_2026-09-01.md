## Core domain rules (owner's law — do not violate)
1. **Priorities**: P1 SAP → P2 LIM-TOS → P3 LIM-LD. LD gets the leftover fleet.
2. **Contractor walls**: BLB pit = RIM only, KR = SMA, TF open. Trucks never change owner.
3. **SAP routing**: each pit sends ~2,000 t/day to its POS buffer (BLB→POS 14, KR/TF→POS 12), rest DIRECT to FeNi.
4. **POS is transit**: inbound tonnes leave on IWIP trucks, input = output. IWIP is not contractor fleet (`foreign:true`).
5. **One clock**: never mix before/after-adjustment numbers in one table. Month targets must sum to the sales line every surface divides by.
6. **The YEAR is the judged line** (103% band). Fleet adjustment = **PARK ONLY** at ONE flat level all year (the purchase-decision number). Never redistribute surplus into short months.
7. Fleet never shrinks: Sep 581 / Oct 931 / Nov 1280 / Dec 1281 (RIM 961 + SMA 320 in Dec). All remaining trucks go on LD past target.
8. **Scenario = day slot**: day-01=S1, 03=S3.0.1, 04=S3.0.2, 05=S3.1.1, 06=S3.1.2, **07=S7 (4.1)**. One workbook = one scenario.
