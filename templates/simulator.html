6 · Production estimate per haul

Trip time is the weigh-to-weigh interval a planner recognises. Effective cycle is shift-minutes per completed trip — measured per route, and what trips/shift divides by, because it also covers the empty return, the shovel queue and breaks. Roster is how many trucks to have on shift to keep that many hauling, from measured availability; a starred figure means no availability is measured for that route's trucks, so the site-wide prior is used.
