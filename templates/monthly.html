What the three numbers are

Prediction

The minimum you can bet on tomorrow. Measured trips/DT × this fleet, capped at the most trips that road has ever done in a day. Every variable in it is already in the history — it does not wait for a new scenario.

Achievable

What the same fleet can deliver if loader and dump point ceilings hold. This is the simulate engine on the Plan tab (Check capacity). When conditions change, run a new scenario there and rebuild — the two numbers are never averaged.

Target

Your matrix: WMT/day × days in the month. The plan you wrote. Same figure every day — nothing is invented between days.
