📅 Monthly plan — prediction vs manual

← back to simulator

0 · Yearly plan matrix one paste fills every month

Paste (or upload) the mine-planning matrix — ORIGIN / MATERIAL / DESTINATION / CONTRACTOR rows with WMT/day and NB_DT_ per month. Each month then builds with one click: manual side = the matrix's own WMT/day × days, prediction side = the same fleet (NB_DT_) run through the measured plan engine.

1 · Prediction plan from the Plan tab

Pick a saved daily plan. It runs every day of the month: day = 2 × 12 h shifts × the plan's per-shift prediction. Same fleet, same paths, rain fixed at the saved value.

2 · Manual plan your Excel

Upload the month plan you made by the usual method (historical trips, no variables) — .xlsx or .csv with a date (or day number) column and a WMT/tonnage column. Or paste rows.

3 · Comparison

The export contains the daily table, totals, the plan basis (paths, DT, WB, material, rain) and three native Excel charts.
Daily table

Prediction days come from the saved plan's own totals (measured path engine, shared ceilings, weighbridges). The manual plan is stored verbatim — nothing is recalculated on that side. To model a different month, save another daily plan in the Plan tab and build from it here.
